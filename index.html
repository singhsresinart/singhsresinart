From Your Idea to a Keepsake You’ll Treasure.

A simple, personal process — from your first WhatsApp message to the finished piece.

01

Share Your Idea

Send us your photos, flowers, names, dates, inspiration or simply tell us what you have in mind. We’ll understand what you want to create.

02

Plan Your Design

We discuss the size, shape, colours, layout, personalization and other details. You’ll know the design direction and price before we begin.

03

We Handcraft It

Once everything is confirmed, your piece is carefully handcrafted with attention to the details that make it yours. For flower preservation orders, your flowers are prepared and incorporated into the agreed design.

04

Your Keepsake Is Ready

After the final checks, your finished piece is carefully prepared for pickup or delivery. We’ll keep you updated as your order progresses.

The People Behind the Pieces

Made by us. Made for your memories.

Singh's Resin Art is a husband-and-wife studio based in Hyderabad, started by Sagar & Preethi in May 2023 as a side hustle.

What began as a creative passion slowly became something much more meaningful — a way to preserve flowers, photographs, milestones and memories in pieces that can be kept for years.

Today, every piece is still made with the same personal approach: we work closely with you, understand what the memory means to you, and carefully turn your idea into something tangible.

— Sagar & Preethi
